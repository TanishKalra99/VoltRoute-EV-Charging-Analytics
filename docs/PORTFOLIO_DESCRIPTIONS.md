## LinkedIn Projects section (copy-ready)

**VoltRoute — EV Charging Network Analytics | Excel, Power Query, PivotTables**

Developed an interactive Excel dashboard using 1,000 synthetic EV charging-session records and a 36-station master dataset. Built a repeatable Power Query data-quality process to identify duplicate IDs, invalid stations, missing completed-session energy and negative durations, separating 954 analysis-ready records from 46 exceptions. Created slicer-driven KPIs with GETPIVOTDATA and analysed completion rates, city-wise energy, monthly demand and exception categories. Documented findings and recommendations for targeted reliability investigation.

**Tools:** Excel, Power Query, PivotTables, PivotCharts, XLOOKUP, COUNTIFS, SUMIFS, AVERAGEIFS, GETPIVOTDATA.
